# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone who contributes, reports issues, reviews changes, or uses the package.

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Focusing on the technical issue rather than the person.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Reporting concerns privately when public discussion could cause harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Sexualized language or attention that is not welcome.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional technical community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate for behavior that violates this policy.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not use public issues or pull requests for sensitive reports.

All reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
